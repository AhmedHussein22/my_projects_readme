# 🌱 Shoura Online - شورى أونلاين

Agricultural products and public health supplies, delivered across Egypt.

Shoura Online connects customers with original products from Shoura Chemicals, a distributor and representative of leading international agrochemical manufacturers. Browse more than 400 products, including pesticides, agricultural fertilizers, and seeds, alongside sprayers, traps, and agricultural supplies.
